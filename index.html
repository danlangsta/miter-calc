ℹ️ Why does the keeper side flip on the saw? ▼

When you flip a board upside-down onto the saw (ceiling edge flat on the bed, wall edge flush against the fence), left and right ends trade places. A Left Wall Piece is held on the Right of the blade, and a Right Wall Piece is held on the Left of the blade.
